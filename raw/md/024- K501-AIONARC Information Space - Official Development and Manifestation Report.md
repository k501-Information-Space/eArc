## Ontology Meaning

### Axiom

Represents foundational truth.

Purpose:

Defines the root principle from which ontology emerges.

---

### Frame

Represents a bounded information structure.

Purpose:

Creates identifiable units of information.

---

### StateObject

Represents the condition of a Frame.

Purpose:

Captures information at a specific moment.

---

### Snapshot

Represents a recorded StateObject.

Purpose:

Preserves temporal state.

---

### Freeze

Represents immutability.

Purpose:

Locks a Snapshot against modification.

---

### Archive

Represents long-term preservation.

Purpose:

Transforms frozen states into archival artifacts.

---

### Lifecycle

Represents valid transitions.

Purpose:

Defines legal movement between ontology states.

---

### Kernel

Represents orchestration.

Purpose:

Acts as the root coordination object of the ontology.
