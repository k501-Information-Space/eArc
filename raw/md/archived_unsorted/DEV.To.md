Title:

K501: Building a deterministic, append-only information space

Text:

I’m developing a system called K501.

K501 is an append-only information space designed for structured, reproducible knowledge.

It follows a simple model:

- no overwrite

- deterministic structure

- full traceability

Every change creates a new state.

Nothing is removed.

The focus is not on building another AI system, but on creating a stable knowledge layer that AI and data systems can rely on.

The architecture is stable, implementation is evolving.
