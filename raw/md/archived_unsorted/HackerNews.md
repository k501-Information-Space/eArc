Title:

K501: append-only information space for structured knowledge

Text:

K501 is an append-only information space.

It stores knowledge without overwrite, preserving every state in a deterministic structure.

The goal is long-term, reproducible knowledge representation and stable input for AI systems.

The architecture is stable, implementation is evolving.
