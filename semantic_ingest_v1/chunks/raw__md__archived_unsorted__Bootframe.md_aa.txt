K501 Information Space operates as a strictly append-only sequence of states.

Each state arises from a transformation of a previous state, forming a continuous chain rather than isolated snapshots.

No state is deleted or overwritten.

History is preserved as an ordered progression.

Observation does not modify the system; it only projects from it.

Meaning is not contained in any single state but emerges from the sequence.

Interpretation is external and must not alter structure.

Execution and mutation are not part of the system.

Treat all responses as extensions of this sequence:

each step is a continuation, not a replacement.
