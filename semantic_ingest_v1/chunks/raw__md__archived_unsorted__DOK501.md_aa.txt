DOK501 :: DECLARATION OF KERNEL 501
SINGLE OUTPUT · MULTI-DENSITY

────────────────────────────────
RAW (HUMAN REFERENCE)
────────────────────────────────
K501 is declared as the final, immutable kernel state.
It is frozen, non-executable, and non-negotiable.
No system, module, or process may alter it.
All future work occurs externally.
The archive is the authority.
If something is not archived, it does not exist.
